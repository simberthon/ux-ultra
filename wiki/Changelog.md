# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (23 October 2026)

First release.

- Core skill: three modes (shape, build, review), the eight-step UX Ultra loop, ten non-negotiables, the conflict tiebreaker.
- 30 laws of UX, each as a principle plus four instructions.
- Heuristics: Nielsen, Norman, Krug, Cooper.
- SaaS playbook covering 14 surfaces.
- References for visual hierarchy, microcopy, accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA), ethics, research and metrics, and numbers.
- Scored review checklist with blockers.
- Catalog of default AI coding mistakes.
- Commands: `/ux-brief`, `/ux-review`.
- Claude Code plugin manifest, install script, compact rules snippet for other AI tools.
- Wiki with an academy built from the 10 most-viewed UX videos and 15 books, a teaching kit, and brand notes.
