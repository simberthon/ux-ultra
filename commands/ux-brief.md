description: Shape a screen or flow with UX Ultra before any code is written.
argument-hint: "[what you are about to build]"
---

Use the `ux-ultra` skill in **shape** mode for: $ARGUMENTS

Write a short UX brief, then stop and wait for the user's go before coding:

1. **User and goal**: who lands here, and the one job they came to do.
2. **Shortest path**: the steps from arrival to done, numbered. Cut every step you can.
3. **Primary action**: the one dominant action per screen, and where it sits.
4. **States**: empty, loading, partial, error, success, and first-run. One line each.
5. **Risks**: where users will hesitate, make a mistake or lose work, and how the design prevents it.
6. **Done moment**: what the user sees when they have succeeded, and what happens next.
7. **Laws in play**: the 3 to 5 UX Ultra rules that matter most here, and any conflict between them with the tiebreak you chose.
